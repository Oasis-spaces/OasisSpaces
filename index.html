How it works

Four steps from a video to a room you can change.

  1. 01

    Film

    Walk slowly around the room with your phone, 30 to 90 seconds, keeping the walls and floor in frame. No special hardware.

  2. 02

    Rebuild

    We recover where the camera was for every frame, measure depth in metres, and find the walls, the floor and each piece of furniture. The result is to scale, not a guess.

  3. 03

    Edit

    Drag pieces across the floor, take things out, turn or resize them, and add new ones from the library. Every change stays in proportion to the real room.

  4. 04

    Redesign

    Ask for a new look. Suggestions are grounded in your room — its size, its light, what already fits — and you can walk through the result before you commit.

    Questions

    Before you film.

    What do I need to film?
    A phone, and 30 to 90 seconds. Walk slowly, keep the walls and floor in frame, avoid spinning on the spot, and finish where you started so the ends of the video connect.
    Does it work in any room?
    It works best in rooms with some texture and even light. Bare white walls, mirrors and large windows are harder. When a capture is not good enough, we say so and tell you what to film again, rather than hand you a poor model.
    How accurate is the model?
    It is measured in metres from your video, so proportions and placement are right and the room is the size it really is. Check any dimension that matters for a purchase with a tape measure before you buy.
    What can I add to the room?
    Beds, seating, tables, storage, lighting and plants from the library, placed to scale. The library grows with what people ask for, so if a piece is missing, tell us.
    What happens to my video?
    It is used to build your room and for nothing else. You can delete the video and the model whenever you like.
    When can I use it?
    Soon. We are finishing the first release now; the previews on this page show what the editor and the redesign will do. If there is something you would want it to handle, tell us in the suggestion box below.
